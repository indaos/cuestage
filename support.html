CueStage Presenter Support

Need help? Email support@daos.cloud and we'll reply within two working days.

Which permissions does CueStage need?

Screen Recording, to show your slide window on the stage. Camera, Microphone and Speech Recognition only for the features that use them. Turn them on in System Settings › Privacy & Security, then quit and open CueStage again.

How do I share my stage in a video call?

Click Output in CueStage, then share the window "CueStage Output" in Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams or any other app. Or use CueStage Camera (below).

How do I use CueStage Camera?

In CueStage, click Camera › Install CueStage Camera, then allow it in System Settings › General › Login Items & Extensions › Camera Extensions. Click Camera › Send stage to CueStage Camera, and select "CueStage Camera" as the camera in your meeting app. If the picture looks blurred, turn off the meeting app's background blur or effects.

How do I restore my purchase?

Click Get Pro in CueStage, then Restore Purchases, using the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your Mac, open the App Store, click your name, then Account Settings › Subscriptions › Manage. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Is my content uploaded?

No. Your windows, videos, voice and camera stay on your Mac. See the privacy policy.
