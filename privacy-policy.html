CueStage Presenter Privacy Policy

Effective: 23 October 2026

CueStage Presenter shows your slides, videos, camera, titles and logos on a stage for presentations and video calls on your Mac. It is built so that your content never leaves your Mac.

Data we collect

None. CueStage has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not send your windows, videos, voice, camera picture or any personal information to us or to anyone else.

Screen Recording

CueStage asks for Screen Recording permission so it can show the windows you choose (for example your slides) on the stage. The pictures are used live on your Mac and are not stored, except in recordings that you start and save yourself.

Camera and microphone

The camera is used only when you add a camera area. The microphone is used only when you turn on Voice or record with sound. Nothing is stored, except in recordings that you start and save yourself.

Speech recognition

Voice triggers and the teleprompter use Apple's speech recognition on your Mac. If your Mac cannot recognize speech on the device, Apple's speech service may process the audio; Apple's privacy policy applies to that service. Matching phrases by meaning uses Apple's on-device models.

CueStage Camera

The optional CueStage Camera shows your stage as a camera. Only the apps in which you select CueStage Camera receive this picture.

Your files

Videos and images you add stay where they are. CueStage keeps a reference so it can open them again after a restart. Your layouts and scripts are stored in the app's private storage on your Mac. Recordings are saved where you choose.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to those transactions. The number of free Pro trial presentations you used is stored only on your Mac.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
